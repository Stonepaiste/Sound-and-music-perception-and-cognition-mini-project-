Energy and Aggression in the Music of Sleep Token

Mathias Jaqué, 2026

Background

Researchers have struggled to explain what listeners get out of music that communicates negative emotions such as sadness or aggression. Non-fans often report negative experiences with heavier forms of metal, where loud sound levels and certain timbral qualities (distorted guitars, low-tuned riffs, harsh vocals) are associated with aggression. Yet the same features can also convey high energy, which is linked to arousal rather than negative feeling.

Why Sleep Token

Sleep Token blends progressive metal and djent with R&B, pop and ambient music, often moving from soft, intimate passages to very heavy sections within a single track. This makes it interesting to see if people perceive different parts of the songs differently.

Method

The project has two parts:

  • A musical analysis of the selected tracks (loudness, tempo, spectral and timbral features, and where heavy and soft sections occur).
  • A listening test where fans and non-fans rate perceived energy, aggression and emotional response.
